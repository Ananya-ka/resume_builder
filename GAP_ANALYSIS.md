## Prioritization decisions

1. **Restore a truthful vertical slice first** (F01–F04, F14–F16, F25): reuse backend review/discovery in the current light UI; do not invent replacement endpoints merely to make buttons appear functional.
2. **Make the catalog authoritative** (F07–F09, F15, F18–F19): candidate identity, source spans, immutable claim versions, explicit approval.
3. **Make objections and four buckets actionable** (F10–F13): grounded requirement mappings; absence is unknown until clarified, not a proven deficit; unknown can be a qualification status without becoming a fifth gap bucket.
4. **Use approved claim versions everywhere** (F16–F17, F21): drafts, PDF, defense cards, and corrections share the same lineage.
5. **Keep ATS and analytics secondary** (F20, F22): preserve their utility without allowing them to define evidence truth or a hiring guarantee.

No finding requires a wholesale rewrite or a new microservice platform. Retain the current UI design, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Celery, parser/extractor code, conservative verifier, templates, and working legacy interactions; refactor their boundaries and contracts.
